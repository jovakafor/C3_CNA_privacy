Privacy Policy

C3 SNA app · Last updated 15 November 2026

Who we are

The C3 SNA app is made by Joel Jeka (jovakafor@gmail.com) for C3 Southern Africa.

What we collect

  • Church details: church name, country, city, leader name and leader contact number.
  • Sunday reports: numbers of adults, children, visitors, members, those who received Christ, and offering amount. We do not collect names of children or members.
  • Edit requests: the Sunday and the reason you type.
  • Administrator accounts: email address and password.
  • Device sign-in: a random anonymous ID so a church phone stays linked to its church.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone. The app has no adverts and no tracking or analytics tools. We do not sell your data.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to show a church its own statistics, to show administrators the totals and levy for each country, and to handle edit requests.

Where it is stored

Data is stored securely online with Supabase, a database and sign-in service. A church can only see its own data. Administrators can see all churches. People with the view key can only see statistics.

Permissions on your phone

  • Internet: to send and receive reports.
  • Date and time: to know today's date for Sunday reports.
  • A folder you choose: to save PDF, Excel and Word files. The app only writes to the folder you pick.

Sharing

We share data only with Supabase (to store it) and with the C3 administrators of the app. We do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Until the church or administrator asks for it to be removed. The main administrator can erase all online data from the app.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing jovakafor@gmail.com. We will reply as soon as we can.

Children

The app is for church leaders and is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, the date above will change.
